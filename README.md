# Social Media Usage, Electronic Bullying, and Adolescent Mental Health
## Introduction
This project summarizes and visualizes the relationship between youth social media usage, electronic bullying, and mental health.

## Data Description
The dataset contains data recorded for a sample of 322 13-18 year olds from the CDC’s 2023 Youth Risk Behavior Surveillance System (YRBSS) survey study, a nationally representative health study of U.S. adolescents.

## Statistical Analysis
Generating descriptive statistics in order to determine the relationship between mental health score values and social media use.

## Synthesized Results
The proportion of individuals in the dataset who indicated they have ever been electronically bullied is 18.32%. 

There is a 0.0212, or 2.12% probability, that 3 or more adolescents in the sample will report having been electronically bullied. 

Among more frequent social media users between ages 13-18 years old who took the CDC’s 2023 Youth Risk Behavior Surveillance System (YRBSS) survey study, the prevalence of experiencing electronic bullying was 19.6%. Among less frequent social media users between ages 13-18 years old who took the CDC’s 2023 YRBSS survey study, the prevalence of experiencing electronic bullying was 13.9%. 
The probability that one randomly sampled person from the dataset being a more frequent user of social media but not reporting experiencing electronic bullying is 0.6242, or 62.42%.
